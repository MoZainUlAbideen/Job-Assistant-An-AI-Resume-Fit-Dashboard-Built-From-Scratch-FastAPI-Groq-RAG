MLOps Engineer
Location: Islamabad (Onsite)

The role
Own the platform that takes our computer vision models from notebook to production.

You will:
• Package models with Docker and deploy them to Kubernetes clusters on GCP
• Build CI/CD for model training using GitHub Actions
• Monitor model performance with Prometheus and Grafana
• Optimize inference with ONNX and TensorRT on NVIDIA GPUs (CUDA)

You have:
• At least 3 years of experience in DevOps or MLOps
• Strong Python and Bash; comfortable on Linux
• Experience with Terraform
• Bachelor's in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering or similar

It's a bonus if you have:
• Experience with Kubeflow or MLflow
• Exposure to edge deployment on Jetson devices
